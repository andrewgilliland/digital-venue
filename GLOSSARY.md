# Digital Venue

Digital Venue describes the physical seating of a venue and the event-specific offers available within it.

## Language

**Venue**:
A physical place containing fixed seating areas.
_Avoid_: Stadium, building

**Deck**:
A vertical tier of sections within a venue.
_Avoid_: Level, bowl

**Section**:
A named seating area within a deck containing one or more rows.
_Avoid_: Zone, block

**Row**:
An ordered line of seats within a section.

**Seat**:
A stable, individually identifiable place in a row, independent of any event.
_Avoid_: Ticket

**Event**:
A scheduled occasion at a venue for which offers may be available.

**Offer**:
Event-specific availability and pricing for one or more seats.
_Avoid_: Ticket, listing

**Selection**:
The seats and offer a visitor has chosen during the current session.
_Avoid_: Reservation, hold, cart
